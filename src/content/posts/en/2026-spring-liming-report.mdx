# Spring 2026 Liming Trip Report

Late March in Liming turns the sandstone into caramel by mid-afternoon. This was our first Liming trip of the year — five climbers from Chengdu, Shanghai, and Hong Kong, ranging from a strong 5.9 leader to a steady 5.11 second.

## Highlight routes

- **Thousand Turtle Wall · Slow Dance (5.10c, 3P)** — three of us on-sighted it.
- **Flag Cloud Wall · Daybreak (5.10a)** — a good first-real-multi-pitch objective.
- **Camel Hump Wall · Untitled (5.11a)** — one send after two burns.

## What we learned

- Early-spring temps swing hard. Afternoon wind kicks in around 3 PM — plan photos and long rests around noon.
- Both village guesthouses will do dinner on request; message a day ahead.
- The trail network between the Lisu villages deserves an afternoon on rest day.
