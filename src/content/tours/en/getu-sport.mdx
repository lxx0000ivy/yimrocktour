# Getu Big Cave Sport Climbing

Just across the Yunnan border in western Guizhou, Getu hides one of the most striking cave-climbing venues in the world. The Great Arch tops out over 100 meters and is stacked with sport routes — from friendly 5.10s to serious 5.13 test-pieces.

## What we do

- **Day 1** · Land in Anshun, transfer to Getu, settle in.
- **Days 2 – 3** · Single-pitch sport at the entrance crags, dial in tufa and dripstone technique.
- **Days 4 – 5** · Multi-pitch sport lines inside the Great Arch, 5.10c – 5.11d.
- **Day 6** · Send day or visit a Bouyei village; return to Anshun in the evening.

## Who this is for

- Comfortable leading 5.10a sport outdoors
- Fine with steep, overhanging terrain and long pitches
- Climbers looking for a big-cave, big-wall sport experience
