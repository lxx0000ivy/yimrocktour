# Kunming Real Rock · Fly Me To The Moon

Fly Me To The Moon is one of the closest outdoor crags to Kunming — friendly angles, dense routes, and a good first day on real stone.

## What we do

- **Half-day / full-day** · 1 – 1.5 hr drive from Kunming to the crag.
- Harness and helmet fit, gear check.
- Top-rope belay calls and standard terminology.
- Multiple top-rope routes with coaching (5.9 – 5.11).
- Crag etiquette and risk awareness.

## Who this is for

- Anyone curious about outdoor climbing — gym experience not required.
- Gym climbers looking to step onto real rock for the first time.

## Fine print

Price covers guiding, group gear, and transport to and from the crag. Meals and personal gear are not included. Group discounts for 3+ climbers.
