# Liming Red Sandstone Trad Tour

Liming, in the mountains northwest of Lijiang, is one of China's premier trad climbing destinations. Red sandstone splitter cracks, multi-pitch lines, a wide grassy basecamp — the place is practically built for trad climbers.

## What we do

- **Day 1** · Drive from Lijiang (~3 hrs) to Liming village, gear check, crack primer.
- **Days 2 – 3** · Single-pitch routes in the 5.9 – 5.10a range. Refresh cam placements, anchor building.
- **Days 4 – 5** · Classic multi-pitches — think three-pitch 5.10s on the Thousand Turtle Wall.
- **Day 6** · Send day or rest day. Optional hike into Laojun Mountain, visit a Lisu village.
- **Day 7** · Return to Lijiang.

## Who this is for

- Comfortable leading 5.9 sport
- Familiar with basic gear placements and anchors (we can add a pre-trip workshop if not)
- Climbers looking to send their first real multi-pitch on gear

## Fine print

Price covers guiding, group gear, village lodging, breakfasts and dinners, and transport between Lijiang and Liming. Flights, personal gear, and insurance are not included.
