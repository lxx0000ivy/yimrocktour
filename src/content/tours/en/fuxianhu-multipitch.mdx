# Fuxianhu Multi-pitch · Jifeng & Tandong

The limestone walls above Lake Fuxian are one of Yunnan's best multi-pitch classrooms — pitch lengths that make sense, well-equipped anchors, and the lake staring back at you from every belay.

## What we do

- **Day 1** · Drive from Kunming to Lake Fuxian (~1.5 hrs). Refresh multi-pitch gear, rope management, and standard calls. Climb Jifeng in the afternoon.
- **Day 2** · Climb Tandong. Review anchor building, cleaning, and double-rope rappel. Return to Kunming in the evening.

## Who this is for

- Already leading at 5.10c or above.
- Climbers who want their first real multi-pitch, or a hands-on review of the full multi-pitch workflow.
- Personal gear required (harness, helmet, shoes).

## Fine print

Price covers guiding, group gear, and transport between Kunming and Lake Fuxian. Meals and lodging are not included. Two-person group discount available.
